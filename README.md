# Aetheria-System-Unbound

AETHERIA: SYSTEM UNBOUND — six rings, one Sanctuary, a tower of 999 floors.

An open-world LitRPG sandbox where you become what you practice: swing a sword, Swordsmanship grows; forage, Herbalism levels. No classes, no locks — only the build you earn.

⚔️ 7 WEAPON STYLES — sword, dagger, greatsword, warhammer, staff, bow, unarmed
🔮 5 MAGIC DISCIPLINES — Elemental, Summoning, Void, Spacetime, Celestial
🌍 6 BIOME RINGS — Emerald Expanse to Desolation Core, Lv 1–90
🩸 BLOODLINE AWAKENINGS — rare lineages that reshape your build
🏠 LIVE YOUR WAY — homesteads, farming, cooking, forging, fishing, trading
🥇 CONSENSUAL DUELS — friendly or gold wagers, non-lethal finishes
👑 WORLD BOSSES + MONOLITH — 999 floors, opens at Lv 100
🗺️ WORLD CODEX — atlas, waystones, gates, hidden nodes

🖥️ ONE CLEAN HUD — Tab stats, M map, B backpack.

No class. No cap. Just the System.
